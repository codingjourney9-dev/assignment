DEPLOYMENT GUIDE — Railway.app (Free Node.js + MySQL Hosting)
=============================================================

WHAT YOU NEED:
- A GitHub account (free) → https://github.com
- A Railway account (free) → https://railway.app

═══════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 1 — Upload files to GitHub
═══════════════════════════════════════════

1. Go to https://github.com → Login or Sign up
2. Click the "+" button (top right) → "New repository"
3. Name it: assignment10
4. Keep it Public → Click "Create repository"
5. On the next page, click "uploading an existing file"
6. Drag and drop THESE 3 FILES from this ZIP:
     ✅ index.js
     ✅ package.json
     ✅ .gitignore
7. Click "Commit changes"

═══════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 2 — Deploy on Railway
═══════════════════════════════════════════

1. Go to https://railway.app
2. Click "Login" → "Login with GitHub" (easiest)
3. Click "New Project"
4. Click "Deploy from GitHub repo"
5. Select your "assignment10" repo
6. Railway will start deploying — wait ~1 minute

═══════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 3 — Add MySQL Database
═══════════════════════════════════════════

1. In your Railway project, click "+ New"
2. Click "Database" → "Add MySQL"
3. Wait ~30 seconds for it to spin up
4. Click on the MySQL service → go to "Variables" tab
5. You will see: MYSQLHOST, MYSQLUSER, MYSQLPASSWORD, MYSQLDATABASE, MYSQLPORT
   ✅ These are automatically shared with your app — NO changes needed!

═══════════════════════════════════════════
STEP 4 — Get your public URL
═══════════════════════════════════════════

1. Click on your Node.js service (assignment10)
2. Go to "Settings" tab
3. Under "Networking" → click "Generate Domain"
4. You get a free URL like: assignment10-production.up.railway.app
5. Open it in browser — your site is LIVE! 🎉
